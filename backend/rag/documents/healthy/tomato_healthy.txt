Plant: Tomato
Condition: Healthy
Care: Provide adequate sunlight, consistent soil moisture without waterlogging, good drainage, and sufficient airflow. Water near the soil surface when possible.
Prevention: Monitor leaves and stems regularly, remove dead plant material, keep tools clean, and maintain appropriate spacing.
General Guidance: Healthy tomato plants should be monitored regularly so that changes in leaf color, shape, spots, or growth can be detected early.

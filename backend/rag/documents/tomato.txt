Plant: Tomato
Watering: Keep soil evenly moist but not waterlogged. Check the top layer before watering.
Sunlight: Usually prefers about 6-8 hours of direct sunlight.
Soil: Well-drained fertile soil.
Common problems: Early blight, late blight, leaf mold, nutrient deficiency.
Care: Improve airflow, avoid wetting leaves unnecessarily, remove severely infected leaves, and keep tools clean.

General Plant Care
Yellow leaves can have multiple causes including overwatering, poor drainage, insufficient light, nutrient imbalance, natural aging, or disease.
Before watering, check the soil moisture rather than watering on a fixed schedule alone.
Good drainage and appropriate light are important for most houseplants.
Fertilizer should be selected according to the plant type and applied according to label directions.
If disease is suspected, isolate the affected plant where practical and avoid sharing unclean tools.

Plant: Corn
Disease: Northern Leaf Blight
Symptoms:
Long, elongated gray-green to tan lesions can develop on corn leaves and may expand significantly under favorable conditions.
Management:
Monitor plants regularly and manage infected crop residue according to local agricultural recommendations.
Prevention:
Crop rotation, residue management, and appropriate plant spacing can help reduce disease pressure.
Plant Care:
Maintain adequate soil moisture and balanced nutrition and avoid prolonged wetness on foliage where practical.

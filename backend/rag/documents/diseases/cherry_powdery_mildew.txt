Plant: Cherry
Disease: Powdery Mildew
Symptoms:
A white, powdery fungal growth may appear on leaves and young shoots. Infected growth can become distorted or weakened.
Management:
Remove severely affected plant material where practical and improve airflow around the plant. Avoid excessive humidity around foliage.
Prevention:
Maintain good spacing and airflow, monitor new growth regularly, and avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.
Plant Care:
Provide appropriate sunlight, well-drained soil, and balanced moisture.

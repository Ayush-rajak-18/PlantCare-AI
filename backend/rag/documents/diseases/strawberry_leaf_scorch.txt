Plant: Strawberry
Disease: Leaf Scorch
Symptoms:
Small dark purple, reddish, or brown spots can develop on strawberry leaves. Spots may enlarge and cause affected tissue to appear scorched.
Management:
Remove severely affected leaves where practical and maintain good airflow. Keep the growing area clean.
Prevention:
Avoid prolonged leaf wetness, maintain appropriate spacing, remove infected plant debris, and monitor plants regularly.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, well-drained soil, and consistent moisture without waterlogging.

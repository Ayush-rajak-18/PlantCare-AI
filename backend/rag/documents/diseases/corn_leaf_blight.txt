Plant: Corn
Disease: Leaf Blight
Symptoms:
Leaf blight can produce brown or tan lesions on corn leaves. Severe infection can reduce photosynthetic leaf area.
Management:
Monitor affected plants and manage infected crop residue appropriately. Follow local agricultural recommendations for disease control.
Prevention:
Use healthy planting material, maintain crop rotation where appropriate, and reduce conditions that favor prolonged leaf wetness.
Plant Care:
Maintain adequate soil moisture and balanced nutrition.

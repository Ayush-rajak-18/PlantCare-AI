Plant: Grape
Disease: Leaf Blight
Symptoms:
Leaf blight can produce discolored or necrotic areas on grape leaves and may reduce healthy leaf area when infection is severe.
Management:
Remove severely affected plant material where practical and improve canopy airflow. Avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.
Prevention:
Maintain vineyard sanitation, suitable plant spacing, and good airflow. Monitor leaves regularly.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, well-drained soil, and appropriate irrigation.

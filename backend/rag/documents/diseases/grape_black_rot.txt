Plant: Grape
Disease: Black Rot
Symptoms:
Small reddish-brown lesions may develop on leaves and berries. Infected berries can darken, shrivel, and develop black fruiting structures.
Management:
Remove infected berries and plant debris where practical. Improve canopy airflow and avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.
Prevention:
Maintain good vineyard sanitation, appropriate spacing, and airflow. Remove mummified fruit and infected debris.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, well-drained soil, and appropriate irrigation without prolonged leaf wetness.

Plant: Tomato
Disease: Leaf Mold
Symptoms: Pale yellow areas may develop on the upper leaf surface, with olive-green to brown mold-like growth commonly appearing on the underside.
Management: Improve ventilation and reduce humidity around foliage. Remove severely affected leaves and avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
Prevention: Provide adequate plant spacing, improve airflow, and water at the soil level when possible.
Plant Care: Keep foliage dry and inspect both sides of leaves in humid growing conditions.

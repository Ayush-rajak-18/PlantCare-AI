Plant: Tomato
Disease: Early Blight
Symptoms: Dark brown lesions with concentric ring patterns commonly develop on older leaves. Severe infection can cause yellowing and leaf drop.
Management: Remove badly affected leaves, improve airflow, and avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
Prevention: Rotate crops where practical, remove infected plant debris, and keep foliage from contacting wet soil.
Plant Care: Water at the soil surface when possible and monitor lower leaves regularly.

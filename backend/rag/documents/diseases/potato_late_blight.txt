Plant: Potato
Disease: Late Blight
Symptoms:
Water-soaked or dark lesions may develop on leaves and stems. Under favorable conditions, lesions can expand rapidly and infected tissue may die.
Management:
Remove heavily infected foliage where practical and follow local agricultural recommendations for disease management. Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
Prevention:
Use healthy planting material, maintain good field sanitation, improve airflow, and avoid unnecessary overhead irrigation.
Plant Care:
Keep soil adequately moist without waterlogging. Provide full sun and well-drained soil.

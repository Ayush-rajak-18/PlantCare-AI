Plant: Tomato
Disease: Spider Mites
Symptoms: Fine webbing, stippled or speckled leaves, yellowing, and loss of leaf vigor may occur, especially during hot and dry conditions.
Management: Inspect leaf undersides and reduce plant stress. A strong but appropriate water spray can help dislodge mites on suitable plants.
Prevention: Avoid excessive plant stress and dusty conditions, and regularly inspect plants during hot, dry weather.
Plant Care: Maintain appropriate moisture and check the undersides of leaves for mites and webbing.

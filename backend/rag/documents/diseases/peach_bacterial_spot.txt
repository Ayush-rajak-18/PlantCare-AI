Plant: Peach
Disease: Bacterial Spot
Symptoms:
Small dark spots can develop on leaves and fruit. Severe leaf infection may cause shot-hole symptoms and premature leaf drop.
Management:
Remove severely affected plant material where practical and maintain good airflow. Follow local agricultural recommendations for bacterial disease management.
Prevention:
Use healthy planting material, maintain sanitation, and avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, well-drained soil, and appropriate irrigation.

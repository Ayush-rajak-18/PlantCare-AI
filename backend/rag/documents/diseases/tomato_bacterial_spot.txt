Plant: Tomato
Disease: Bacterial Spot
Symptoms: Small dark water-soaked spots may appear on leaves, stems, and fruit. Spots can enlarge and become rough or scabby.
Management: Remove severely affected plant material and keep foliage as dry as practical. Improve airflow and avoid working with plants when leaves are wet.
Prevention: Use clean planting material, sanitize tools, provide adequate spacing, and avoid unnecessary overhead irrigation.
Plant Care: Monitor new growth regularly and maintain good drainage, sunlight, and airflow.

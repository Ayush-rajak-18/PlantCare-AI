Plant: Corn
Disease: Gray Leaf Spot
Symptoms:
Gray to tan rectangular lesions can develop on corn leaves, often expanding along the leaf veins.
Management:
Monitor disease development and remove or manage infected crop residue according to local agricultural guidance.
Prevention:
Crop rotation and residue management can reduce disease pressure. Maintain suitable plant spacing and field airflow.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate moisture and balanced nutrition while avoiding unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.

Plant: Tomato
Disease: Septoria Leaf Spot
Symptoms: Numerous small circular spots may develop on older leaves, often with pale centers and darker margins. Severe infection can cause leaf drop.
Management: Remove affected lower leaves and plant debris, improve airflow, and minimize leaf wetness.
Prevention: Provide adequate spacing, avoid overhead irrigation when possible, and remove infected debris after the growing season.
Plant Care: Inspect older leaves regularly and maintain good ventilation around plants.

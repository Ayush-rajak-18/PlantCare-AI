Plant: Grape
Disease: Esca
Symptoms:
Esca is associated with wood-decay fungi and may cause leaf symptoms, reduced vine vigor, and decline of affected grapevines.
Management:
Remove severely affected or dead plant material where practical and maintain vineyard sanitation. Follow local agricultural guidance for management.
Prevention:
Use healthy planting material, make careful pruning cuts, and avoid unnecessary wounds to the vine.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, appropriate irrigation, and good canopy airflow.

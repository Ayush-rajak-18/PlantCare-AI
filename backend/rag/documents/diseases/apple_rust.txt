Plant: Apple
Disease: Rust
Symptoms:
Rust diseases can cause yellow, orange, or reddish lesions on leaves and may reduce plant vigor when infection is severe.
Management:
Remove heavily affected plant material where practical and improve airflow around the plant.
Prevention:
Maintain sanitation, good airflow, and avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
Plant Care:
Grow in full sun with well-drained soil and provide adequate moisture without waterlogging.

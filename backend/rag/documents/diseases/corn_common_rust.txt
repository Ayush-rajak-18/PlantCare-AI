Plant: Corn
Disease: Common Rust
Symptoms:
Small reddish-brown to rust-colored pustules may appear on corn leaves. Severe infection can reduce healthy leaf area.
Management:
Monitor plants regularly and manage the disease according to local agricultural recommendations. Remove severely affected material where practical.
Prevention:
Use healthy planting material, maintain appropriate plant spacing, and avoid conditions that keep foliage wet for long periods.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, suitable soil moisture, and balanced nutrition.

Plant: Pepper Bell
Disease: Bacterial Spot
Symptoms:
Small water-soaked or dark lesions may develop on leaves, stems, and fruit. Severe infection can cause leaf loss and blemished fruit.
Management:
Remove severely affected plant material where practical. Avoid working with wet foliage and maintain good airflow.
Prevention:
Use healthy seed or planting material, maintain sanitation, avoid overhead irrigation when possible, and avoid unnecessary leaf wetness.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, well-drained soil, and consistent moisture without waterlogging.

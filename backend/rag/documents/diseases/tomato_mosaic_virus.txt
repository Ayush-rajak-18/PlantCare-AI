Plant: Tomato
Disease: Mosaic Virus
Symptoms: Leaves may show mottled light and dark green patterns, distortion, reduced growth, or irregular development.
Management: Remove strongly affected plants where appropriate and prevent mechanical spread through contaminated hands and tools.
Prevention: Use clean planting material, wash hands and tools, control potential insect vectors where relevant, and avoid handling healthy plants immediately after infected plants.
Plant Care: Monitor new leaves for mosaic patterns and unusual distortion.

Plant: Apple
Disease: Apple Scab
Symptoms:
Olive-green to brown spots can develop on leaves and fruit. Severe infection may cause leaf drop and affected fruit.
Management:
Remove and dispose of fallen infected leaves and affected fruit. Improve airflow by appropriate pruning. Avoid unnecessary leaf wetness.
Prevention:
Maintain good orchard sanitation, improve airflow, and avoid prolonged moisture on foliage.
Plant Care:
Provide full sun and well-drained soil. Water the root zone rather than repeatedly wetting the leaves.

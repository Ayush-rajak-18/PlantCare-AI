Plant: Potato
Disease: Early Blight
Symptoms:
Dark brown lesions with concentric ring patterns may develop on older leaves. Severe infection can cause leaf yellowing and premature leaf loss.
Management:
Remove heavily infected foliage where practical and maintain good field sanitation. Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
Prevention:
Practice crop rotation, use healthy planting material, maintain appropriate plant nutrition, and reduce prolonged moisture on foliage.
Plant Care:
Keep soil consistently moist during tuber development but avoid waterlogging. Provide full sun and well-drained soil.

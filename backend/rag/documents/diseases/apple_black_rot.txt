Plant: Apple
Disease: Black Rot
Symptoms:
Dark, enlarging lesions can occur on leaves, fruit, and branches. Fruit may develop dark rot and eventually shrivel.
Management:
Remove infected fruit and plant material. Prune affected areas where appropriate and maintain good airflow.
Prevention:
Keep the growing area clean, remove fallen or diseased material, and avoid unnecessary leaf wetness.
Plant Care:
Provide full sun and well-drained soil. Maintain appropriate watering and avoid waterlogging.

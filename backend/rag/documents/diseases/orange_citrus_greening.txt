Plant: Orange
Disease: Citrus Greening
Symptoms:
Citrus greening can cause yellowing or mottled leaves, reduced tree vigor, and small or poorly colored fruit. Symptoms can vary across the tree.
Management:
Monitor trees regularly and follow local agricultural recommendations for management. Control of the insect vector and removal of severely affected trees may be recommended in affected production areas.
Prevention:
Use healthy, certified planting material where available and monitor for insect vectors. Follow regional citrus disease-management guidance.
Plant Care:
Provide adequate sunlight, appropriate irrigation, good drainage, and balanced nutrition.

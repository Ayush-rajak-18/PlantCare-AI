Plant: Squash
Disease: Powdery Mildew
Symptoms:
White, powder-like fungal growth can appear on leaves and stems. Severely affected leaves may become yellow, dry, or weakened.
Management:
Remove severely affected leaves where practical and improve airflow around plants. Follow local agricultural recommendations for disease management.
Prevention:
Provide adequate plant spacing, good airflow, and avoid excessive conditions that favor disease development.
Plant Care:
Provide suitable sunlight, consistent soil moisture, and well-drained soil. Avoid unnecessary prolonged leaf wetness.
